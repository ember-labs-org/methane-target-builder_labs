Where the data comes from

Emissions and fossil-fuel abatement data come from the IEA. For agriculture and waste, which lack country-level cost curves, the tool uses global ratios from UNEP. Rows built that way are labelled in the tool.

Costs use the IEA's per-gigajoule figures. More details in the methodology (PDF).

Where the cuts come from

Drag the white handle on any bar to set how much of that sector you want to abate. The pale band behind it shows the technical potential, shaded by what it costs, and the dashed line shows the maximum abatement currently possible for that sector.
